Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 5, 2026

Summary

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data. Steam Daily is a client-side puzzle game. Your gameplay data stays on your device.

Data We Don't Collect

Steam Daily does not collect or store any of the following: personal information (names, email addresses, IP addresses), gameplay statistics or history, cookies or tracking identifiers, analytics or behavioral data, or any data you enter during gameplay (your guesses, scores, etc.).

Local Storage

Steam Daily may use your browser's local storage to save your progress on the current day's puzzle so you can return to it. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

Third-Party APIs

Game data displayed in puzzles is fetched from publicly available APIs (Steam Store API and SteamSpy API). These requests are made server-side and do not expose your personal information to those services.

External Links

Steam Daily contains links to Steam store pages. When you click these links, you leave our site and are subject to Steam/Valve's privacy policy.

No Accounts

Steam Daily does not have user accounts, login systems, or registration. There is no mechanism for us to identify individual users.

Children's Privacy

Steam Daily does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, it will be updated on this page with a new date. Since we collect no data, changes would be minimal.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue on our project repository.
